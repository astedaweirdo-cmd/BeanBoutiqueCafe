About Us

More than just a coffee.

We believe that a great coffee shop is a place where you can take a break from your life, meet friends, create new memories and enjoy the moment.

Bean Boutique was founded on March 12, 2021, by the owner, Phyo Myat Yati. Since then, it has grown into a group of 15, all committed to maintaining the quality and care that Bean Boutique is known for. From amazing cups of coffee to inspiring workshops and memorable events.
